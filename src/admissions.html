Admissions – Fall 2026

COMSATS University Islamabad is now accepting applications for undergraduate and postgraduate programs for Fall 2026.

Open Programs

  • BS Computer Science (4 Years)
  • BS Software Engineering (4 Years)
  • BS Electrical Engineering (4 Years)
  • BS Information Technology (4 Years)
  • BS Cyber Security (4 Years)
  • MS Computer Science (2 Years)
  • MS Data Science (2 Years)
  • PhD Computer Science

Eligibility Criteria

  • BS Programs: Minimum 60% marks in FSc (Pre-Engineering / Pre-Medical / ICS) or equivalent. Valid Entry Test score (SAT / MDCAT / NTS / CUI Entry Test).
  • MS Programs: Minimum 16 years of education (BS/BEng) with at least 2.5 CGPA or 60% marks from an HEC-recognized institution.
  • PhD Programs: MS/MPhil degree in relevant discipline with at least 3.0 CGPA. GRE / NTS subject test may be required.
  • International students must provide equivalency certificates from IBCC / HEC.

How to Apply

  • Step 1: Visit the official admissions portal at admissions.comsats.edu.pk.
  • Step 2: Create an account and complete the online application form.
  • Step 3: Upload required documents (transcripts, CNIC, passport photos, entry test result).
  • Step 4: Pay the non-refundable application processing fee of PKR 2,000 via bank challan or online payment.
  • Step 5: Submit the application and keep the tracking ID for future reference.
  • Step 6: Appear for the CUI Entry Test on the designated date if applicable.
